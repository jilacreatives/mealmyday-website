2. The key points at a glance

  • To use MealMyDay you need an account, with an e-mail address and password, in the Android app also with your Google account and in the iPhone app with your Apple ID (section 4).
  • Your data is stored on servers in Frankfurt am Main (EU).
  • For your weekly plans we need health data such as weight, calorie goal or allergies. We store it in your account only with your explicit consent. Consent is voluntary: without it, your details and weeks stay on your phone only. You can consent or withdraw at any time (section 5).
  • The app and this website contain no advertising, no tracking and no analytics services. We do not sell data and do not pass it on for advertising.
  • We advertise MealMyDay on Facebook and Instagram. In doing so, we only receive aggregated figures from Meta, no data about individual people (section 14).
  • Our AI receives no data from you. It only helps us draft recipes in advance.
  • Premium is a subscription, monthly or yearly, via Google Play or the App Store. The first week is free, without a subscription and without payment details. We never see your payment details (section 7).
  • You only get notifications if you allow them on your phone. The app schedules them on your device, so nothing is sent to a push service (section 3).
  • You can export your data in the app as a PDF, and you delete your account yourself in the app.
  • MealMyDay is for people aged 16 and over (section 4).

3. The app, notifications and data on your device

Loading recipes

When it starts, the app loads the recipes and foods from our database at Supabase (servers in Frankfurt am Main). In doing so, Supabase processes technically necessary data such as your IP address, the time of the request and the app version. No account or information about you is needed for this.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is to provide the app in a working and secure way.

What the app stores on your device

The app only stores the following on your device:

  • your sign-in: an access key with your account ID and e-mail address and, when signing in with Google or Apple, also the information from section 4 (for example your name), so that you don't have to enter your password every time you start the app – until you sign out,
  • your choice between the light and dark theme,
  • whether the app has already asked you for permission to send notifications, so that the question only comes up once,
  • how many jars your pantry shelf (“Vorratsregal”) has already shown on this device, so that new jars are placed on the shelf only once,
  • whether you have skipped the weekly question about your weight this week,
  • whether you have already seen the short tour after setup,
  • if you have not consented to the storage of your health data (section 5): your details, your weight history and the content of your weeks and of your last ten past weeks – per account.

This storage is strictly necessary for the functions you have requested (Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG, German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act). No consent is required for this. We cannot see what is stored on your device without consent. It remains until you consent (the app then transfers it to your account and deletes it from the device), delete your account or delete the app – it is also gone when you change phones. The app does not include this data in your phone's backup (Google or iCloud backup).

Notifications

If you allow it, the app lets you know as soon as your new week is unlocked (“Deine neue Woche ist fertig” – Your new week is ready) and reminds you of your pantry shelf on the last day of a week. Your phone asks for this permission once, in its own window. You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone's settings.

The app schedules the notifications on your device from the data of your weeks. No data is sent to us, to Google, to Apple or to any other push service for this. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Links

Links in the app, for example to the sources of the nutritional values or to this website, open in your browser. The privacy notices of the respective provider apply there.

Apart from the permission for notifications, the app does not ask for any permissions, so no location, no camera, no microphone and no contacts. It does not use advertising identifiers.

4. Account and sign-in

To use MealMyDay you need an account. For this we process:

  • E-mail address – as your username and for confirmation and security messages,
  • Password – only in encrypted form (as a hash value). We do not know your password in plain text. If you sign in with Google or Apple, there is no password.
  • Account ID – a random number under which your data is stored,
  • Timestamps of your registration, of the confirmation of your e-mail address and of your last sign-in,
  • Signed-in sessions – for each active sign-in, the IP address and the type of device or app (technical identifier of the program, “user agent”), in order to detect misuse,
  • First name – optional, only used to address you in the app. It is not health data and remains stored even after you withdraw your consent (section 5), until you change it or delete your account.

When you register and when you use “Passwort vergessen” (Forgot password), we send you a code by e-mail. We send these e-mails from the mailbox support@mealmyday.app at IONOS (Germany).

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of the contract of use). For the session data, additionally Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is to protect your account from unauthorised access.

Minimum age: MealMyDay is for people aged 16 and over. From the age of 16, you can consent to the processing of your data yourself (Art. 8 GDPR). If you enter a younger age, the app does not complete the setup and does not plan any weeks.

Signing in with Google

In the Android app, instead of using an e-mail address and password, you can also sign in with your Google account (“Weiter mit Google” – Continue with Google). You then select a Google account on your phone, and Google confirms the sign-in to us with a signed proof. In doing so, we receive from Google:

  • the e-mail address of your Google account and whether Google has verified it,
  • your name as it appears in your Google account and, where applicable, the address of your profile picture,
  • an identifier of your Google account.

We store this information at Supabase with your account. We use the e-mail address in the same way as when you sign in with a password. If there is already an account with the same verified address, you are signed in to that account. During setup, the app suggests your first name for addressing you. You can change it or leave it out. The app does not display your profile picture. We have no access to other data in your Google account, such as contacts or files.

For the sign-in itself, Google (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) is an independent controller. In the process, Google learns that you are signing in to MealMyDay. The Google Privacy Policy applies. When you sign out of the app, it also signs you out with Google. You can also disconnect MealMyDay from your Google account at any time in your Google account under “Third-party apps & services” (German: “Drittanbieter-Apps und -Dienste”).

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Signing in with Google is optional. You can also use MealMyDay with an e-mail address and password.

Signing in with Apple

In the iPhone app, you can sign in with your Apple ID instead of an e-mail address and password (“Weiter mit Apple”, Continue with Apple). You confirm this on your iPhone, for example with Face ID, and Apple confirms the sign-in to us with a signed token. In the process, we receive from Apple:

  • an identifier that Apple issues for MealMyDay only,
  • an e-mail address: that of your Apple ID or, if you choose “Hide My Email”, a relay address from Apple that forwards messages to you,
  • only at the first sign-in and only if you share it, your name.

We store this information at Supabase with your account and use it in the same way as when you sign in with Google. The free month for the waiting list is only available for the same e-mail address, so not with a relay address from Apple. For the sign-in itself, Apple (Apple Distribution International Limited, Hollyhill Industrial Estate, Hollyhill, Cork, Ireland) is an independent controller. The Apple Privacy Policy applies. You can disconnect MealMyDay at any time in your iPhone settings (Apple Account → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple).

When you delete your account, you confirm once with Apple. With the one-time code that Apple issues for this, we ask Apple to revoke the sign-in for MealMyDay. We do not keep any sign-in data from Apple for this. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Signing in with Apple is optional.

5. Your details and health data

So that MealMyDay can calculate your calorie requirements and plan your weeks to suit you, you enter the following in the app:

  • sex, age, height, weight and activity level,
  • goal (lose weight, maintain weight, build muscle), focus and, where applicable, your own calorie goal and a target weight,
  • diet (for example vegetarian, vegan or halal),
  • allergies and intolerances,
  • food preferences (for example “mehr Gemüse” (more vegetables), “Fleisch höchstens zweimal pro Woche” (meat at most twice a week), ingredients you don't like),
  • meals per day, “nur schnelle Gerichte” (quick dishes only) and smaller portions.

Weight, target weight, calorie goal, allergies and intolerances are health data and are therefore specially protected (Art. 9 GDPR). Other details, taken together, can also reveal something about your health. Your diet may, under certain circumstances, allow conclusions to be drawn about religious or philosophical beliefs, for example in the case of “halal”. We therefore treat all of these details with the same strict care.

Weight history

We store every weight entry with the date in your weight history, at most one entry per day and at most 400 entries. The oldest entries are then dropped. From this, the app shows you your weight curve in your profile. If your goal is to lose weight or to build muscle, the app asks you for your weight once a week. You can skip the question.

In your account only with your explicit consent

We only store these details, your weight history and the content of your weeks (section 6) in your account if you have explicitly consented. The app asks for this consent separately during setup. The legal basis is Art. 9(2)(a) in conjunction with Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR.

Consent is voluntary – you can use MealMyDay just the same without it. Your details, your weight history and the content of your weeks then stay on your phone only (section 3): they survive closing the app and signing out, but are gone if you delete the app or change phones – the app then asks for them again. With consent, they are available on every device, even after changing phones. You can also consent later in your profile under “Gesundheitsdaten” (Health data). You can delete your account at any time (section 8).

As proof (Art. 7(1) GDPR), we store when you consented, which wording you saw and in which version, and, where applicable, when you withdrew your consent. The legal basis for this is Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR. If we change the substance of the consent text, the app asks you once more. If you do not consent then, this counts as a withdrawal.

Withdrawal

You can withdraw your consent at any time in the app's profile under “Gesundheitsdaten” (Health data). We will then immediately delete from your account your stored details, the content of your weeks (dishes, quantities and the ticks on your shopping list), your weight history and saved plans from older app versions. Anything that is not health data remains stored until your account is deleted: your first name, your shopping day, the periods of your weeks and your pantry shelf, the swap log, your favourites and your subscription. Your details, your weight history and the content of your weeks remain on your phone, and you can continue to use MealMyDay. The withdrawal applies to the future. Processing carried out up to that point remains lawful.

What we use the details for

Only to calculate your calorie and nutrient requirements and to plan and display your weeks. We do not use them for advertising, do not pass them on to third parties (except to our hosting provider Supabase, section 15) and do not transmit them to AI services. The calculation is a guideline and is no substitute for medical or nutritional advice.

6. Weeks, swapping and pantry shelf

MealMyDay plans week by week, in line with your shopping day. For this we store:

  • Your weekly rhythm: your shopping day, your phone's time zone (so that your new week is unlocked at 8 a.m. your local time), the start of your first week (which is also the start of your free week, section 7) and when you last unlocked a week in advance. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
  • Your weeks: the start, end and type of each week, when it was unlocked, when you opened it and ticked something off on the shopping list, and how it counts towards your pantry shelf. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
  • The content of your weeks: the planned dishes with quantities, the dishes of the preview weeks, the ticked-off items on your shopping list and changes after a swap that have not yet been confirmed. We only store this in your account with your consent from section 5 (Art. 9(2)(a) and Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR); otherwise it stays on your phone (section 3). Of past weeks, the content of the last ten is kept (“Vergangene Wochen” (Past weeks) in your profile); for older weeks, the database or the app deletes it automatically.
  • Swapping and learning: When you swap a dish, we store which dish you swapped out, its main ingredients and when. From this, the app learns which dishes it should no longer suggest to you and which ingredients it should suggest less often. We also store anything you allow again under “Was die App über dich gelernt hat” (What the app has learned about you). This is not health data. We keep the last 500 swaps in the log; “Alles zurücksetzen” (Reset everything) in your profile deletes it. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
  • Favourites: name, type and calories of the dishes you save with “Merken” (Save). The app suggests them to you a little more often. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
  • Pantry shelf and rewards: Your pantry shelf is based on your weeks. When it is full, we store which reward you redeemed and until when it is valid (section 7). With a yearly subscription you get a gift code for a friend: we store the code, until when it is valid and whether and when it was redeemed. We store who redeemed it only internally, so that it cannot be used twice. You cannot see this, and your friend cannot see who the code came from. If you share the code via “Teilen” (Share), you decide with whom and via which app. To prevent anyone from guessing codes, we store only the account and the time of incorrectly entered codes and delete this after one day. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; for the failed attempts, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is to prevent misuse.
  • Older app versions: Before the week model, MealMyDay created individual plans. For app versions that still work this way, we store the time, length and tier (not the content) of each plan created, in order to check the number of new plans, and, with your consent, your most recently used plans (at most ten with Premium, otherwise two). Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; for the plans, Art. 9(2)(a) and Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR.

7. Subscription and purchases

Premium is a subscription, monthly or yearly, taken out via Google Play or the Apple App Store. It renews automatically until you cancel it in the store's subscription settings. Payment, cancellation and refunds are handled by the respective store, acting as an independent controller (section 10). We do not receive payment details such as bank account numbers or credit card details.

Free week

When you create your weeks for the first time, you get 7 days of Premium – without a subscription and without payment details. It ends automatically. For this, our server counts from the start of your first week (section 6), once per account. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Rewards and gift codes

You get a reward for a full pantry shelf. With a monthly subscription on Google Play, your next payment is charged one month later. For this, our server passes a new expiry date to Google via RevenueCat. If you don't have a renewing subscription, you instead get one month of Premium unlocked. With a yearly subscription you get a gift code (section 6). Anyone who redeems a gift code gets one month of Premium without a subscription, but only if no subscription is currently running. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

RevenueCat

To manage subscriptions and purchases, we use the service RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA) as a processor. When it starts, the app connects to RevenueCat to retrieve the price in your currency and your tier, even if you don't buy anything. In doing so, RevenueCat processes:

  • an identifier: if you are signed in, your account ID, otherwise a random identifier from RevenueCat. RevenueCat does not receive your e-mail address or your health data.
  • Subscription and purchase data from Google or Apple: product (monthly or yearly), purchase and expiry date, whether the subscription renews or has been cancelled, refunds and transaction numbers where applicable; also unlocks without a purchase (free month from the waitlist, free month for a full pantry shelf, gift month) with start and end,
  • technical data such as IP address, language, store country and currency, operating system as well as operating system and app version.

Our server asks RevenueCat whether you have Premium and records this in your account: tier, store or unlock, whether it is a yearly subscription, whether it has been cancelled and until when it runs.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of the contract for your subscription). If you don't buy anything: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is to show you the correct price and your current tier. For the transfer to the USA, see section 16.

Sales statements

Google and Apple send us statements about sales (for example order number, product, country, where applicable postcode and amount, but without name and e-mail address). We keep these in accordance with our tax obligations (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR in conjunction with Section 147 of the German Fiscal Code (Abgabenordnung), for up to ten years).

8. Data export and deleting your account

Under “Meine Daten exportieren” (Export my data) in your profile, the app loads the data in your account, including the information Google or Apple sent us when you signed in (without consent also, marked as such, your details, your weight history and the content of your weeks from your phone), and creates a PDF file from it on your device. The app cannot retrieve your active sessions (IP address, device type) or a waitlist entry – we will tell you about these on request by e-mail. Nothing additional is stored on our server for the export. The file remains in the app's cache until you create the next export, restart the app, sign out or delete your account. You decide whether and where you save or share it before then.

Under “Konto löschen” (Delete account) in your profile, you delete your account immediately and permanently after a confirmation prompt, with all settings, consents, weeks, the swap log, the weight history, favourites, rewards and gift codes, the tier and the plan count of older versions. The app also deletes everything it has stored for your account on your phone. We also delete your customer record at RevenueCat. If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke the sign-in with Apple (section 4). This does not end a running subscription – cancel it in the subscription settings of Google Play or of your Apple account. Google and Apple keep purchase receipts according to their own rules. “Konto löschen” does not delete a waitlist entry (section 13) – use the unsubscribe link in our e-mails or write to us. You can also delete your account by e-mail, see Deleting your account and data.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR in conjunction with Arts. 15, 17 and 20 GDPR.

9. Dishes with AI

We draft new recipes in advance with the help of an AI (Claude by Anthropic) and, after a review, store them in our own recipe database. We do not calculate the nutritional values with the AI, but from the German Nutrient Database (Bundeslebensmittelschlüssel, BLS).

The app does not use AI and does not send any of your data to an AI. Your plan is calculated in the app from the recipe database. Anthropic receives neither your details nor your e-mail address.

13. Waitlist

On the home page you can sign up for the waitlist with your e-mail address. We will then send you an e-mail with your free month of Premium when the app launches. We will not send you any other promotional e-mails or a newsletter.

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). We use double opt-in: after signing up, you receive an e-mail with a confirmation link. Only when you click it are you on the list.

We store:

  • your e-mail address,
  • the times of your sign-up, your confirmation and our e-mails to you,
  • the wording and version of the consent you agreed to, as proof (Art. 7(1) GDPR),
  • a random code for the confirmation and unsubscribe links,
  • whether and when you redeemed the free month,
  • where you came from, if the link to the website had a source tag, for example “meta/ig/3-einkaufszettel” after a click on our advertising on Instagram. This lets us see how many sign-ups an ad brings in. We do not pass this information on to anyone, not even to Meta. The legal basis for this is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is to measure the success of our advertising.

We do not store your IP address in the process. We also do not measure whether you open our e-mails or click on links in them.

Signing up via a form on Facebook or Instagram: In some of our ads, you can also sign up directly in a form from Meta, without opening our website. There you enter your e-mail address (Meta suggests the address from your account for this) and tick the same box as on our website. The information in the form is initially stored by Meta. We download the entries at most once a day, add them to our waitlist and then send you the confirmation e-mail. Only after your confirmation are you on the list. From Meta we only receive your e-mail address, the time and which ad you came through (for example “meta-formular/ig/3-einkaufszettel”). We delete the downloaded file immediately after importing it. Meta's own processing is governed by the Meta Privacy Policy. For the transfer to the USA, see section 16.

Free month: If, after the launch, you register in the app with the same e-mail address, our server matches it against the waitlist and unlocks one month of Premium for you once via RevenueCat, redeemable up to three months after the launch. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (fulfilling our promise).

Service providers: We store the data at Supabase (servers in Frankfurt am Main). When you submit the form, your browser sends the address directly to Supabase. In doing so, Supabase also technically processes your IP address (section 15). We send the e-mails from the mailbox support@mealmyday.app at IONOS.

Retention period: We delete unconfirmed sign-ups after 30 days. Confirmed sign-ups remain stored until you unsubscribe, at the latest until three months after the app's launch, when the free month can no longer be redeemed: one week after that, we delete the entire waitlist.

Unsubscribing: You can withdraw your consent at any time, via the unsubscribe link in every e-mail or by e-mail to support@mealmyday.app. We will then delete your entry immediately. The withdrawal applies to the future.

  • Data on your device (section 3): the sign-in until you sign out, the PDF file of a data export until the next start of the app (section 8), everything else until you delete the app.
  • Technical logs: at Supabase a few days, at GitHub according to its own rules.
